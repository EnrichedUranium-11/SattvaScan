Ingredient of the Week

INS 407 (Carrageenan)

A seaweed-derived thickener used in dairy/plant milks. Recent clinical audits link it to gut inflammation and digestive distress. Clean brands are swapping it for guar gum or packaging without stabilizers.

Why is Maida harmful?
Maida (refined wheat flour) is stripped of all wheat germ and fiber. This causes rapid spikes in blood glucose levels, taxing insulin secretion and increasing diabetes risks over time.
What makes Palmolein oil poor?
Palmolein/Palm oil is heavily refined and contains ~50% saturated fats. It is cheap and stable for shelf life, but excessive consumption raises LDL cholesterol and arterial blockages.
